Our Story

Two crafts, one counter.

Grove & Grind started as two separate carts at the Sunday farmers market — one pressing juice, one pouring coffee. In 2018 we put them under one roof, because the same instinct runs through both: buy real ingredients close to home, handle them gently, and serve them the same day.

Every juice is pressed hydraulically, cold, within hours of your order. Every coffee is roasted in eight-kilogram batches on our drum roaster in the back window, where you can watch it happen. Nothing here is designed to sit on a shelf.

Sourced Close

Produce and dairy from farms within 150 miles; green coffee from direct-trade partners we've visited.

Made to Order

Honest Labels

How It's Made

From grove to glass. From bean to brew.

Two parallel processes, run out of the same kitchen, on the same daily clock.

Grove to Glass

Source

Produce arrives daily from farms within 150 miles — nothing pre-cut or refrigerated for more than a day.

Wash & Trim

Every piece is hand-washed and trimmed on-site, the same morning it's pressed.

Cold-Press

A hydraulic press extracts juice without heat or high-speed blades, preserving more nutrients and flavor.

Bottle & Chill

Bottled to order and chilled to 34°F immediately. No pasteurization — best enjoyed within three days.

Bean to Brew

Source

Direct-trade relationships with growers in Ethiopia, Kenya, Guatemala, and Colombia — visited in person.

Roast

Small eight-kilogram batches on our drum roaster, roasted in-house three times a week.

Rest

Beans rest five to ten days after roasting so the flavor has time to develop properly.

Brew

Dialed in fresh every morning by the barista on bar, then brewed to order — never batch-held.
